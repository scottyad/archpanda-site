Privacy Policy

How Obsidian Citation Capture handles your data
2 min · Scott

Privacy Policy#

Effective Date: September 12, 2026

Overview#

Obsidian Citation Capture is designed with privacy as a core principle. We minimize data collection and give you full control over where your data goes.

What Data We Collect#

Extension Usage (Local Only)#

  • Capture history — Stored locally in your browser
  • Settings/preferences — Stored in browser localStorage
  • License key — Stored locally to verify your subscription tier

Cloud AI Features (Optional)#

If you use the Pro+ Cloud AI tier:

  • Paper abstracts — Sent to our managed proxy for Claude summarization
  • No personal data — We do not collect your name, email, or browsing history
  • Ephemeral processing — Summaries are generated and returned; we do not retain paper content

Payment Data#

  • Stripe handles all payments — We never see your credit card information
  • License assignments — We store which license key was assigned to which Stripe transaction (for customer support)

What We Do NOT Collect#

  • ❌ Your browsing history
  • ❌ Personal identification information
  • ❌ Your Obsidian vault contents
  • ❌ Your API keys (BYOK mode keys stay in your browser)
  • ❌ Analytics or tracking data

Third-Party Services#

  • Stripe — Payment processing (PCI compliant)
  • Anthropic Claude — AI summarization (via our managed proxy, not direct from your browser)
  • CrossRef/arXiv/PubMed — Metadata extraction (public APIs)

Your Rights#

You can:

  • Export your data — All local data is accessible via browser storage
  • Delete your data — Uninstall the extension to clear all local storage
  • Opt out of Cloud AI — Use local Ollama or BYOK mode instead

Contact#

For privacy questions: privacy@archpanda.xyz
